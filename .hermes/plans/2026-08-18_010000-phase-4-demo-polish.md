## Enhancements to land (priority order)

### P0 — Must-have for demo (3 changes, ~45 min)

1. **Portfolio page** — Build a real-looking portfolio grid with 8 placeholder SVG cards (artist, style, description). Real tattoo site visitors immediately judge by portfolio quality — this is the most important upgrade. No external images, all generated SVGs to avoid copyright/permission issues.

2. **Book page** — Replace "coming soon" with a real consultation form (name, IG/email, tattoo idea, size, placement, preferred dates). This proves the contact-form architecture works on /book. Logs submissions to console.

3. **Contact form** — Make the success message less generic. Currently shows "Message sent!" but in stub mode it should say "Thanks! We'll be in touch within 1 business day" + show what they submitted so they can screenshot it.

### P1 — Polish (5 changes, ~60 min)

4. **Aftercare page** (`/aftercare`) — Tattoo shops universally have this. Adds SEO keywords (aftercare is high-intent search), shows shop expertise. ~7 sections: cleaning, moisturizing, sun exposure, swimming, signs of infection, healing timeline, when to contact.

5. **Real IG grid on home page** — Instead of "Follow us on IG" placeholder, render 6 SVG cards labeled "@ibleedink_600" with mock caption text. Shows the IG integration without needing real API access.

6. **Reviews section on home** — 3 fake review cards (5 stars, name, text, style) labeled as "demo testimonials." Makes the site look lived-in.

7. **Hero with actual art imagery** — Add a backdrop SVG (ink splatter / blood drip / texture) to the hero so it doesn't look empty on desktop with no images. Pure SVG, no licensing.

8. **404 page improvement** — Currently minimal. Add hero + "Page not found" + "Take me home" + "See portfolio" CTAs.

### P2 — Nice-to-have (~30 min)

9. **JSON-LD FAQPage schema** — Auto-generate from `faqs.ts` data on `/faq` page. Boosts SEO significantly.

10. **Sticky CTA on mobile** — Bottom-fixed "Book Now" + "Call" buttons on mobile viewport. Common pattern on tattoo sites.

11. **Breadcrumbs** — Add JSON-LD BreadcrumbList to inner pages. Helps SEO + UX.

12. **Open Graph image variations** — Generate page-specific OG images for `/artists/isiah-jackson`, `/services`, etc. instead of just the default.
