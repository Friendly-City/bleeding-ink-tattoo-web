## Specific Layout Patterns Worth Pulling

| Pattern | Where to find it | Why it works |
|---------|------------------|--------------|
| Hero video / looping tattoo time-lapse | Most modern studios | Sets tone immediately, shows craft |
| Artist spotlight grid on home | Nodedr's coverage | Puts artists front-and-center (they're what clients pick) |
| Style filter chips above portfolio | Common pattern | Visitors narrow by style before scrolling |
| Flash sheet as Pinterest-style grid | GlossGenius examples | Walk-in vibe, set-price clarity |
| Booking CTA sticky on scroll mobile | Most modern | Instagram bio link = phone = must convert |
| Healed-tattoo gallery tab | Nodedr | Shows long-term quality, builds trust |
| Per-artist page with IG embed | Colorlib | Authentic, always-fresh content |

## Out-of-Bounds

> Things we will NOT do, captured during intake or design review.

- ☐ Mimic any specific studio's branding 1:1
- ☐ Use stock tattoo photos anywhere (instant credibility kill)
- ☐ Generic "small business website" template look
- ☐ Autoplay audio on landing
- ☐ Modal popups before scroll
